WorldSport Account Deletion

We are sorry to see you go. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from WorldSport, please follow the instructions below:

Send an email to our support team at support@worldsport.com (or your personal email) with the subject line "Delete My Account" from the email address registered with your account.

Your account and all related personal data will be permanently removed within 48 hours of receiving your request.
